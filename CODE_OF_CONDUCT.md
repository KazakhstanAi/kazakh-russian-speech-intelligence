# Code of Conduct

We are committed to respectful, harassment-free collaboration. Be constructive, respect differences in language and background, protect participant privacy, and critique ideas rather than people.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, doxxing, non-consensual use of voices, impersonation, deliberate privacy violations, and sustained disruption are unacceptable.

Report concerns privately to the repository maintainers through the organisation's GitHub contact channel. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation to protect contributors and research participants. Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible.
